CHICK'TASTIC

Try Our Old New Recipe Of Mouthwatering Crispy Chicken

45 minutes

410 calories

Easy

INGREDIENTS

  • 600 g chicken breast
  • 100 g all-purpose flour
  • 2 eggs
  • 150 g breadcrumbs
    (panko for extra crunch)
  • 1 tsp paprika
  • ½ tsp garlic powder
  • ½ tsp salt
  • ½ tsp black pepper
  • Vegetable oil for frying
  • Lemon wedges and fresh parsley
    for serving (optional)

Instructions

  1. Prepare the chicken Cut the chicken breasts into evenly sized strips. Pat them dry with paper towels so the coating sticks properly.
  2. Prepare the coating Place flour in one bowl, beaten eggs in another, and breadcrumbs mixed with paprika, garlic powder, salt and pepper in a third
  3. Coat the chicken Dip each chicken strip into the flour, then the beaten egg, and finally the breadcrumbs. Press gently so the crumbs stick to all sides.
  4. Fry until golden Heat a layer of vegetable oil in a frying pan over medium heat. Fry the chicken in batches for approximately 3-5 minutes per side, depending on thickness, until golden and fully cooked.
  5. Serve and enjoy Transfer the chicken to a plate lined with paper towels to remove excess oil. Serve warm with lemon wedges, fresh parsley and your favourite dipping sauce.

A LITTLE ADVICE
FROM CHEF

For extra crunchy chicken, let the coated chicken rest for 10-15 minutes before frying. Always check that the chicken is fully cooked before serving.
